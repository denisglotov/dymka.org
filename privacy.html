Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 20, 2026

1. Overview

This Privacy Policy applies to the website dymka.org and mobile applications developed and published by SIA Dymka (Registration No. 50203521431, Latvia). We are committed to respecting your privacy and protecting any personal data processed through our services.

2. Data Collection & Usage

Our website and mobile applications are designed with privacy in mind:

  • Account & Contact Information: If you register an account, sign in, or contact us directly, we may collect information such as your email address, username, or profile identifiers needed to authenticate you and provide account-related services.
  • Technical & Usage Data: When you access our website or use our applications, certain technical and statistical information may be collected automatically (such as browser type, operating system, device model, pages visited, session duration, and approximate geographic region) to ensure technical reliability and improve our services.
  • IP Address & Network Information Processing: To provide core functionality (such as displaying network details in apps like Just IP Info), applications send requests to external IP lookup APIs. Your IP address is processed temporarily and ephemerally by these services solely to return approximate geolocation, ISP, and network parameters. SIA Dymka does not store, track, or profile this data on its own servers.

3. Cookies & Google Analytics

We use Google Analytics (provided by Google Ireland Limited for users in the European Economic Area, and Google LLC in other jurisdictions) on our website and applications to analyze traffic and understand how visitors interact with our content.

Google Analytics uses cookies (such as _ga and property-specific measurement cookies) or pseudonymous identifiers to collect aggregated, statistical data. In accordance with modern analytics standards (Google Analytics 4), IP addresses are masked and anonymized to protect user privacy.

Opt-Out Options: You can manage or block cookies through your browser settings. You can also prevent Google Analytics from collecting and processing your data by installing the official Google Analytics Opt-out Browser Add-on.

4. Advertising

Our mobile applications may display advertisements served by third-party advertising partners, such as Google AdMob. These ad networks may process non-sensitive device identifiers (such as your Android Advertising ID) to deliver non-personalized or personalized advertisements and prevent ad fraud.

5. Purchases & Payment Information

If an application or website offers in-app purchases, digital items, or premium features, all payment transactions are processed securely through authorized third-party payment processors (such as Google Play Billing). SIA Dymka does not collect, receive, or store credit card details, bank credentials, or sensitive financial information on its servers.

6. Third-Party Services

To analyze usage, display advertisements, retrieve network information, or process payments, our website and applications utilize third-party services, including:

  • Google Analytics & Google Play Services (Google Ireland Limited / Google LLC) – for web/app analytics and platform features. Learn more about how Google uses data when you use our partners' sites or apps and the Google Privacy Policy.
  • IP Geolocation & Diagnostic APIs – for network information lookups.
  • Authorized Payment Gateways – for secure digital transactions.

Data processing by these external providers is governed by their respective privacy policies.

7. Data Retention & Security

Personal and account information is retained only for as long as necessary to fulfill the purposes outlined in this policy, provide our services, or comply with applicable legal obligations. Aggregated analytics data collected via Google Analytics is retained according to standard Google Analytics data retention schedules. Any local application preferences or configurations stored on your device remain on your device until cleared or uninstalled.

8. Your Rights (GDPR)

As an EU-based entity in Latvia, SIA Dymka adheres to General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) principles. You have the right to request information about any personal data processed, request its rectification or erasure, or object to processing where applicable. If you have questions regarding data processing or wish to exercise your rights, please contact us.
