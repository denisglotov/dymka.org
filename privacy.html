Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 25, 2026

1. Overview

This Privacy Policy applies to the website dymka.org and mobile applications developed and published by SIA Dymka (Registration No. 50203521431, Latvia). We are committed to respecting your privacy and protecting any personal data processed through our services.

2. Data Collection & Usage

Our mobile applications and website are designed with privacy first:

  • No Personal Data Collection: We do not require account creation, nor do we collect or store personal identifiable information (PII) such as your name, address, or phone number.
  • Device & Usage Data: Basic non-personal diagnostic data or crash reports may be collected automatically by platform services (such as Google Play Console) strictly to improve software stability and performance.

3. Third-Party Services

Our Android applications distributed via the Google Play Store utilize Google Play Services for distribution and standard operating system framework capabilities. Please refer to Google's Privacy Policy for more information on platform-level data handling.

4. Data Retention & Security

Since we do not collect personal user data, we do not store or retain personal data on external servers. Any app preferences or settings stored on your device remain strictly local to your device.

5. Your Rights (GDPR)

As an EU-based entity in Latvia, SIA Dymka adheres to General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) principles. If you have questions regarding data processing or wish to exercise any rights, you can contact us directly.
