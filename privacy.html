Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 28, 2026

1. Overview

This Privacy Policy applies to the website dymka.org and mobile applications developed and published by SIA Dymka (Registration No. 50203521431, Latvia). We are committed to respecting your privacy and protecting any personal data processed through our services.

2. Data Collection & Usage

Our mobile applications are designed with privacy in mind:

  • No User Accounts: We do not require account registration, nor do we collect names, email addresses, phone numbers, or other personal identifiers.
  • IP Address & Network Information Processing: To provide core functionality (such as displaying network details in apps like Just IP Info), applications send requests to external IP lookup APIs. Your IP address is processed temporarily and ephemerally by these services solely to return approximate geolocation, ISP, and network parameters. SIA Dymka does not store, track, or profile this data on its own servers.

3. Advertising & Analytics

Our mobile applications may display advertisements served by third-party advertising partners, such as Google AdMob. These ad networks may process non-sensitive device identifiers (such as your Android Advertising ID) to deliver non-personalized or personalized advertisements and prevent ad fraud.

4. Purchases & Payment Information

If an application or website offers in-app purchases, digital items, or premium features, all payment transactions are processed securely through authorized third-party payment processors. SIA Dymka does not collect, receive, or store credit card details, bank credentials, or sensitive financial information on its servers.

5. Third-Party Services

To retrieve network information, display advertisements, or process payments, applications utilize third-party APIs and platform services (including Google Play Services, ad networks, IP lookup providers, and authorized payment gateways). Processing of requests and data by these external providers is governed by their respective privacy policies.

6. Data Retention & Security

Since we do not collect personal user data, we do not store or retain personal data on external servers. Any app preferences or settings stored on your device remain strictly local to your device.

7. Your Rights (GDPR)

As an EU-based entity in Latvia, SIA Dymka adheres to General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) principles. If you have questions regarding data processing or wish to exercise any rights, you can contact us directly.
