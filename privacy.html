Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 27, 2026

1. Overview

This Privacy Policy applies to the website dymka.org and mobile applications developed and published by SIA Dymka (Registration No. 50203521431, Latvia). We are committed to respecting your privacy and protecting any personal data processed through our services.

2. Data Collection & Usage

Our mobile application is designed with privacy in mind:

  • No User Accounts: We do not require account registration, nor do we collect names, email addresses, or phone numbers.
  • IP Address Processing: To provide the core functionality of displaying your current network information, the app sends a request to an external API service. Your IP address is processed temporarily solely to fetch location and network provider details. We do not store, track, or profile this data on our own servers.

3. Third-Party Services

To retrieve IP and network information, the application communicates directly with the ipinfo.io API service. The processing of your IP address by this service is governed by IPinfo's Privacy Policy.

4. Data Retention & Security

Since we do not collect personal user data, we do not store or retain personal data on external servers. Any app preferences or settings stored on your device remain strictly local to your device.

5. Your Rights (GDPR)

As an EU-based entity in Latvia, SIA Dymka adheres to General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) principles. If you have questions regarding data processing or wish to exercise any rights, you can contact us directly.
